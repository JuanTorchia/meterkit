## Synthetic verification

Automated packed-artifact, registry, database and devnet checks are engineering
evidence only. They are not users, integrations, customers, willingness to pay
or retention. Per-release results belong under `docs/evidence/self-service/`.

## Promotion gate

The primary CTA remains the free, uncompensated five-person beta. A fixed USD
100 founder setup may become a promoted secondary offer only after at least
three independent starts, two unassisted completions, median first-402 below ten
minutes, and one independently controlled finalized settlement with protected
response and replay rejection. Until then it is merely an optional service on
request, not a product subscription and not tester compensation.
