# Self-service promotion gate review

Review date: 2026-08-17

Decision: keep the free five-person devnet beta as the primary CTA. Do not
promote a fixed USD 100 offer, purchase a dedicated domain, or invest in a new
visual identity yet. The optional founder setup may be quoted only when a
customer explicitly asks MeterKit to perform the integration.

Evidence at review time:

- verified external independent starts: 0;
- verified autonomous completions: 0;
- externally controlled settlement + protected response + replay: 0;
- measured external median time-to-402: unknown;
- day-seven eligible observations: 0.

The current `juanchi.dev` subdomain honestly communicates maintainer ownership
and remains adequate for the technical beta. A dedicated domain can reduce
perceived project-person coupling, but it cannot compensate for an initializer,
recovery path or published artifact that has not been independently exercised.

Re-evaluate after at least three independent starts, two completions without a
critical maintainer intervention, median first-402 below ten minutes, and one
complete external settlement/replay chain. At that point re-check name/domain
availability and collision risk before spending money; preserve redirects and
resource identifiers if a domain changes.
