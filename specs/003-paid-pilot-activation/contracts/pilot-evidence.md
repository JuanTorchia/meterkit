# Contract: Pilot and Commercial Evidence

## External activation

A completed external integration requires:

1. immutable `external_independent` participant classification;
2. participant-controlled endpoint and recipient wallet;
3. consent for technical participation and retained private evidence;
4. passing challenge, policy, finalized settlement, protected response and replay
   rejection events;
5. a completion review event referencing minimized evidence.

Invitations, readiness checks, internal dogfood, synthetic agents, compensated
tests and incomplete journeys are reported separately.

## Consent

Consent is recorded independently for participation, evidence retention,
day-seven follow-up, aggregate reporting, public attribution, testimonial and
case study. Denial or withdrawal of one scope does not rewrite another. Public
identity or quotation is never inferred from participation.

## Day-seven retention

Retention is eligible only with an independently completed activation and
permitted follow-up/evidence. `retained` requires an observation on or after
activation plus seven days, supported by participant response or verifiable
valid use. Reporting shows retained, removed, unknown and ineligible counts;
missing evidence stays unknown.

## Willingness to pay and commercial truth

The provider is asked about the exact disclosed offer. Results are stated-price
yes, different-price yes, maybe, no, declined or unknown. Silence, product-model
preference, proposal acceptance and invoice creation are not revenue.

A paid integration and commercial revenue are recognized only after a scoped
cash payment is verified as received with net amount greater than zero. Refunds
reduce net revenue. Devnet assets and grant proceeds are excluded.

## Funding separation

Grant award/tranche status is tracked as funding: eligible, submitted, approved,
payment pending, verified received or rejected. Applicant KYC, wallet proof and
receipts remain private. Grant funds never increment pilot, customer, paid
integration or commercial revenue metrics.

## Compatibility

Activation report v1 remains readable. Its `consentedAt` may map only to private
evidence retention, never public attribution or follow-up. Its repeated-use
window maps to an unverified legacy observation unless dated evidence proves the
seven-day rule. v1 evidence is never silently upgraded to v2 completion.
