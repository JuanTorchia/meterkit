# MeterKit Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community regardless of
experience, identity, background, nationality, language or ability. Technical
disagreement is expected; personal attacks and intimidation are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting corrections.
- Protect privacy and never pressure anyone to disclose wallet, identity or
  financial information.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when reviewing work.
- Respect maintainers' security and scope decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
publishing secrets, deliberate disruption, financial solicitation and deceptive
claims about users, revenue or security are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

For ordinary disagreements, use the relevant issue or discussion. Do not publish
sensitive reports. A confidential conduct concern may be sent to the project
maintainer through the private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub
profile. Platform abuse may also be reported directly to GitHub.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, reject
contributions, issue warnings or temporarily or permanently ban participation.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when
appropriate, and avoid exposing reporters.

This policy applies in the repository, project discussions, community events and
other spaces where someone represents MeterKit.
