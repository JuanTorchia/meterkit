# Trust and limitations

MeterKit never needs a seed phrase or provider private key. Future npm candidates are staged through OIDC and require human 2FA approval. The historical 0.1.0 release does not claim provenance that npm did not expose.

Devnet transactions and automated agents are internal evidence, not revenue or external adoption. Start with the [operating limits](./operations).
